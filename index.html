In your report:

  • • Identify what has been oxidised and reduced for both cell types, support with descriptions of either loss / gain of electrons including the number of electrons or oxidation number changes linked to species.
  • • Discuss the production or use of electrical energy in each cell type.
  • • Write balanced half equations for both cell types and link these to observations.
  • • Write a fully balanced equation for both cell types.
  • • Use reduction potentials to explain your choice of oxidation-reduction processes.
  • • Calculate cell potentials (E0cell) using data provided to predict spontaneity of reaction.
  • • Justify the spontaneity or non-spontaneous nature of each cell with reference to the strength of the oxidant and how this links to the production or use of energy in each cell type.
  • • You may also wish to list the important similarities and differences of each cell type.
